SERVICE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE - FICTIONAL)

This Agreement is made on 1 March 2026 (the "Effective Date") between
Brightline Software Pvt Ltd (the "Vendor") and Kosi Traders Pvt Ltd
(the "Client").

1. Term. This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and expires on
28 February 2027.

2. Renewal. This Agreement renews automatically for one year unless
either party gives written notice at least 60 days before the expiry date.

3. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement for material
breach by giving 30 days written notice. The Client may also terminate
for convenience by giving 90 days written notice.

4. Payment. The Client shall pay each Vendor invoice within 15 days of
receiving it.

5. Reports. The Vendor shall deliver a usage report by the 5th day of
each month.

6. Support. The Vendor shall respond to support requests within a
reasonable time.

7. Non-renewal. Notice of non-renewal must be given at least 30 days
before the expiry date.
